Computer Science Engineering graduate from Jyothy Institute of Technology, Bengaluru. I build generative AI applications — retrieval pipelines and LLM-powered assistants — alongside computer vision systems that have to work in real time.

Most of my work is retrieval-augmented: take a corpus nobody wants to read — HR policy documents, SEBI circulars — and turn it into something a person can ask a question and get a grounded, source-backed answer from. The model call is the easy part. The work is in chunking, retrieval quality, and knowing what to do when the wrong context comes back.

I write pipelines config-driven, so serving a new domain is a configuration change rather than a second codebase. PolicyPilot and FundLens run on the same engine — that was the point of building both.

The other half of what I do is vision: pose estimation, joint-angle maths, and feedback loops fast enough to correct someone mid-rep.
